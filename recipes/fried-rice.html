Tasty Chicken Fried rice

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cooked and cooled white or brown rice (preferably day-old)
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil (divided)
  • 2 large eggs (beaten)
  • 1 cup frozen or finely chopped mixed vegetables (carrots, peas, corn)
  • 2 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Chopped green onions for garnish

Instructions

  1. Add some oil to the pan and fry the egg.
  2. Take the fried egg and add vegitable into the pan.
  3. Season with salt and pepper.
  4. Add the cooked rice into the pan.
  5. Add the fried chiken and egg with
  6. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  7. Mix the sauce with the cooked fried rice.

💡 Chef's Tip

Use cold cooked rice for the best fried rice. Cold rice stays separate while cooking.
