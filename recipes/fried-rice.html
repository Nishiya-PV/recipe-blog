Fried Rice

A quick and delicious fried rice packed with vegetables.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • 1 small onion
  • 1/2 cup carrots
  • 1/2 cup green peas
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a large pan or wok.
  2. Add chopped garlic and onion and sauté for a minute.
  3. Add carrots and green peas and cook until slightly tender.
  4. Add the cooked rice and mix well.
  5. Add soy sauce, salt, and pepper.
  6. Stir-fry everything for 3–4 minutes.
  7. Serve hot and enjoy.
