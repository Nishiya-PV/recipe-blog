Fried Rice

A simple dinner ready in under 30 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cooked and cooled white or brown rice (preferably day-old)
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil (divided)
  • 2 large eggs (beaten)
  • 1 cup frozen or finely chopped mixed vegetables (carrots, peas, corn
  • 2 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Chopped green onions for garnish

Instructions

  1. Prep: Break up cold rice chunks.
  2. Oil: Heat oil in a skillet or wok.
  3. Veggies: Sauté garlic and mixed vegetables.
  4. Eggs: Push veggies aside and scramble 3 eggs.
  5. Rice: Dump in rice and toss until completely hot.
  6. Sauce: Drizzle soy sauce and sesame oil.
  7. Finish: Mix everything together and garnish with green onions.
  8. Garnish with fresh basil and serve.
