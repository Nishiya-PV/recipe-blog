Tomato Pasta

A simple dinner ready in under 30 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 200 g pasta
  • 2 large tomatoes
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • A handful of fresh basil
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Cook the pasta according to the package instructions.
  2. Heat olive oil in a pan.
  3. Add the garlic and cook for one minute.
  4. Add the chopped tomatoes.
  5. Season with salt and pepper.
  6. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  7. Mix the sauce with the cooked pasta.
  8. Garnish with fresh basil and serve.
