Tea

A simple and refreshing cup of tea ready in minutes.

Ingredients

  • 2 teaspoons loose black tea leaves (or 2 tea bags)
  • 2 cups fresh water
  • 1/2 cup milk (optional)
  • 1–2 teaspoons honey or sugar (to taste)
  • A small slice of fresh ginger or lemon (optional)
  • A pinch of cinnamon or cardamom (optional)

Instructions

  1. Bring fresh water to a boil in a kettle or small pot.
  2. Warm your teapot or mug with a splash of hot water, then discard it.
  3. Add the tea leaves to the pot or infuser.
  4. Pour the boiling water over the tea leaves.
  5. Add optional spices or ginger slice to infuse.
  6. Steep for 3 to 5 minutes depending on desired strength.
  7. Strain the tea into your cup and stir in milk or sweetener if desired.
  8. Garnish with a lemon wheel or mint sprig and serve hot.
