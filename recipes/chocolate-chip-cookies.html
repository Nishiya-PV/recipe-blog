Chocolate Chip Cookies

Soft, chewy, and easy to make.

Ingredients

  • 1 3/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 cup dark chocolate chunks
  • 1/2 cup browned butter, cooled
  • 1/2 cup dark brown sugar
  • 1/4 cup white granulated sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt

Instructions

  1. Melt the butter in a pan until it turns golden brown and smells nutty, then let it cool completely.
  2. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  3. Cream the cooled browned butter, dark brown sugar, and white granulated sugar together.
  4. Add the egg and vanilla extract.
  5. Mix in the flour, cocoa powder, baking soda, and salt.
  6. Fold in the dark chocolate chunks.
  7. Place spoonfuls of dough on a baking tray.
  8. Bake for 10–12 minutes.
  9. Cool before serving.
