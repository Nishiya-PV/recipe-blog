🍫 Chocolate Cake

A simple and delicious homemade chocolate cake

Preparation: 15 minutes
Baking: 30–35 minutes
Servings: 8 people

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • ½ cup cocoa powder
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon baking soda
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 2 eggs
  • ½ cup vegetable oil
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 cup milk

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F).
  2. Grease and flour a cake pan.
  3. In a large bowl, mix flour, sugar, cocoa powder, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
  4. Add the eggs, vegetable oil, vanilla extract, and milk.
  5. Mix everything until the batter becomes smooth.
  6. Pour the batter into the prepared cake pan.
  7. Bake for 30–35 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
  8. Allow the cake to cool before removing it from the pan.
  9. Slice and serve.

Tips

  • Do not overmix the batter.
  • Make sure the oven is preheated before baking.
  • Let the cake cool completely before decorating.

Enjoy your homemade chocolate cake! 🎂

---Alternate way---

Chocolate Cake

Soft, tasty and easy to make.

Ingredients

  • Sugar: 2 cups white sugar
  • Flour: 1 ¾ cups all-purpose flour
  • Cocoa Powder: ¾ cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • Baking Powder: 1 ½ teaspoons
  • Baking Soda: 1 ½ teaspoons
  • Salt: 1 teaspoon
  • Eggs: 2 large eggs
  • Milk: 1 cup
  • Oil: ½ cup vegetable oil
  • Vanilla Extract: 2 teaspoons
  • Boiling Water: 1 cup

Instructions

  1. Prep: Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease and flour two 9-inch round baking pans.
  2. Mix Dry Ingredients: Stir the sugar, flour, cocoa, baking powder, baking soda, and salt together in a large bowl.
  3. Add Wet Ingredients: Add the eggs, milk, oil, and vanilla extract. Mix on medium speed with an electric mixer for about 2 minutes.
  4. Add Hot Water: Stir in the boiling water carefully. The batter will look very thin, which is normal.
  5. Pour and Bake: Pour the batter evenly into your prepared pans.
  6. Check Doneness: Bake in the preheated oven for 30 to 35 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
  7. Cool: Let the cake cool in the pans for 10 minutes, then move them to a wire rack to cool completely.
